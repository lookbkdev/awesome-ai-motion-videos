## Original post

> game character with claude opus 5.5 + blender..
> 
> built piece by piece from 3 reference views
> 
> here’s the workflow:
> 
> - step 1: connect opus 5.5 to blender through blender mcp and send 3 reference views (front, close-up, side)
> - step 2: one prompt: “reconstruct this character in 3d. preserve the swept hair and side braid, red cape, ivory tunic and dark boots”
> - step 3: blockout first: boots, legs and overall proportions
> - step 4: body: short tunic, puffed trousers, high boots, relaxed arms
> - step 5: face, swept hair and the braid over the shoulder
> - step 6: details: the cape as one curved surface, then collar, sash, clasp and boot trim
> - step 7: materials in order: dark cloth, leather, ivory tunic, brown hair, scarlet cape
> - step 8: inspect the mesh for seams and overlaps, then compare front and side views with the reference
> 
> get the silhouette right before any detail
> 
> i like to keep the concept art pinned next to the viewport the whole time
