## Original post

> full office in 3ds max with claude opus 5.5...
> 
> claude drives 3ds max directly through mcp
> 
> the trick is building in stages and checking each against the reference. here’s the workflow:
> 
> - step 1: install max ultra mcp (free): drag the installer into the 3ds max viewport
> - step 2: in max ultra’s ai client setup, click claude code to connect it, then run the test prompt
> - step 3: prompt: “rebuild this office in 3ds max. use the reference images, preserve the layout, clutter and daylight”
> - step 4: room shell first: walls, windows, blinds
> - step 5: furniture next, then opus captures the viewport and checks the layout against the reference
> - step 6: shelves and paper stacks, then close-up detail on the chair and desk
> - step 7: assign materials, then match the daylight direction from the window
> - step 8: capture a clean viewport and compare it side by side with the reference
> 
> who does what:
> 
> - max ultra mcp: the bridge between claude and 3ds max
> - opus 5.5: modeling, layout checks, materials, light
> - 3ds max: the scene and the render
> 
> name your renderer in the prompt (v-ray or corona)
> 
> otherwise opus defaults to autodesk physical materials and you redo them all
